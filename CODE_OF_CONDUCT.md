# Code of conduct

We want iamly Beacon to be a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Be
respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and make room
for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/iamlyio/iamly-beacon/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled in a manner proportionate
to the circumstances.
